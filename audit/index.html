Method and limits

What was measured

  • Word ratio: module words ÷ extracted PDF words. A ratio near 1.0 means a full copy.
  • Verbatim score: share of 7-word shingles in a module block that also occur in its PDF.
  • Coverage: word-weighted share of PDF paragraphs represented in the module.
  • Claim checks: every proper noun and number in the digest searched in the source, then read in context.
  • Quiz checks: answer present in the PDF, answer visible in the stem, duplicate options, matching source sentence.

What it cannot prove

  • Figures were not OCR'd. The audit shows that a figure is missing from the site, not whether its caption is accurate.
  • Paraphrase was judged by reading the nearest source passages; subtle changes of emphasis can survive that.
  • PDFs are treated as the authority. Where a PDF is itself a secondary source, the audit checks consistency with it, not independent truth.
